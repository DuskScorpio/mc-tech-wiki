# Tutorial:Tree farming — Minecraft Wiki

**Tree farming** is the process of planting a large number of saplings and waiting for them to grow into trees. These trees are then harvested for logs and more saplings, which can be used to grow another generation of trees. This can be repeated indefinitely, yielding a regular supply of logs without the hassle of covering large areas of terrain, therefore making wood a renewable resource.

## Which type of tree is best?
- **Oak** is plentiful and convenient in compact spaces, making it the best at the beginning of the game. Oak leaves can also drop apples.
- **Birch** grows quickly and has the most uniform height, and is ideal for automatic farming.
- **Dark Oak** and **Pale Oak** grow extremely quick, have a larger average yield than oak, and are considerably more compact and safer to harvest than jungle giants. Dark oak leaves can also drop apples. They are difficult to farm automatically.
- **Jungle** size and its tendency to spawn branches is ideal for mass-production of raw wood yield per tree. The giant variant is difficult to farm automatically.
- **Acacia** is ideal for space-efficient farming. Also difficult to farm automatically.
- **Spruce** is easy to find, but is too tall for convenient harvesting. The giant variant is good for time-efficient wood-quantity.
- **Huge fungus** is only found in the nether and require a nylium block to grow on. Space-efficient; grow only when bone meal is used.
- **Azalea** trees can be farmed for azalea and flowering azalea leaves; yield oak wood without large oak growth risk; fully automatically farmable via moss farms.
- **Mangrove** can be planted underwater and create mangrove roots (extra fuel); bone meal on leaves produces a guaranteed propagule.
- **Cherry** logs grow unpredictably but many saplings drop per tree.
- **Bamboo** can be made into wood; easily farmable.

## Automatic tree farms
Auto grow, manual harvest designs (Minecraft for Dummies, Frilioth's, Mumbo Jumbo's). 1.14 TNT farms; AFK-able universal tree farms; automatic dark oak tree farms; self-sustaining huge fungus farms; AFK-able huge fungus farms. 1.13+ stripped log farms; AFK-able advanced stripped log farm.
