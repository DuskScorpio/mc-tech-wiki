# 20 Engines (GTMC SlimeTech / Twisuki)

Expands "engine" beyond simple flying machines. Definition: anything with *agency* is an engine — a small core or an entire assembly. Goal: go from mounting functional structures onto engines to making functional structures themselves into engines. (Conceptual framing chapter; little mechanical detail beyond the definition.)
