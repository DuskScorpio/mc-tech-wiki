# 21 Mobility (GTMC SlimeTech / Twisuki)

Goal: extend "mobility" into functional structures and make them fly directly (not just mounted on engines).

## Basic mobility structure (from a 10gt flying machine)
Necessary components:
- Two opposing pistons, at least one sticky.
- Two Observers, each activating one piston.
- Slime blocks for connection.

To make a functional structure mobile: fill in those three components. Prioritize reusing the structure's existing slime blocks / observers (don't add new ones if avoidable). Place pistons avoiding the 12-block push limit; ensure all parts connect and move.

The engine need not be 10gt; the principles (two opposing pistons + observers + slime) are what matter. Practice-focused chapter.
