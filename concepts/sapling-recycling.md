# Sapling Recycling

Saplings dropped by [leaf-processing](/concepts/leaf-processing.md) are collected back to the player using hoppers, water flow, or similar.^[raw/articles/gtmc-tree-farm-basics.md]

- **Hoppers** under the farm core feed a dropper the player collects from while AFK.^[raw/articles/gtmc-tree-farm-simple-design.md]
- **Water flow** to gather and flush saplings.
- **Hopper minecarts** — used in fast, tight farms where space is limited.^[raw/articles/gtmc-tree-farm-basics.md]

In 4gt farms the huge item volume means 3–4 droppers throw saplings to the player, and saplings that splash outside the core must be caught with a surrounding wall + hoppers (or water/hopper-minecart flush).^[raw/articles/gtmc-tree-farm-4gt.md]
