# Tree Farm Overview

Tree farms are the foundational topic of Mechanical Redstone. They play an irreplaceable role in basic Technical-Minecraft Survival production, and they are representative of the timing and wiring problems in Mechanical Redstone — which is why GTMC places them at the start of the Mechanical-Redstone volume.[^gtmc-tree-farm-foreword]

## Course structure

GTMC separates the **1×1** tree section from the **2×2** section, to give more targeted explanations of design and operating principles. The curriculum also covers cutting-edge tree-farm results:[^gtmc-tree-farm-foreword]

- **4gt** — all-tree-species farms
- **8gt** — large spruce
- **12gt** — dark oak

…along with some challenging wiring exercises.

## Why start here

Because tree farms exercise the same mechanics the rest of the wiki depends on — growth detection, tile-tick/tile-tick scheduling, and dustless wiring — they are the natural on-ramp to the timing and redstone areas.
