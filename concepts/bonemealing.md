# Bonemealing

Dispensers fire bone meal onto saplings. A dispenser holds little, so bone meal is fed in through hoppers or droppers, with an **unloader** moving it from shulker boxes into the chain.^[raw/articles/gtmc-tree-farm-basics.md]

## High-speed: stacking dispensers

In high-speed farms, bonemealing means **stacking dispensers** — don't worry about consumption. More dispensers widen the *growth window* after a sapling can grow. This introduces **cross bonemealing**: staggering when dispensers fire, generally by 2gt.^[raw/articles/gtmc-tree-farm-high-speed.md]

## 4gt clock: synchronized bonemealing

Because a clock-based 4gt farm has only one growth window every 4gt, it uses **synchronized bonemealing** (not cross), typically a "three-shot + side suction" base.^[raw/articles/gtmc-tree-farm-4gt.md]
